# Q1 — Shopping Cart

Route: `/cart` · Branch: `feature/q1-cart`

Build a product list with a shopping cart.

## Requirements

- Show products from https://dummyjson.com/products with name, price, image and stock.
- Add products to the cart, change quantities and remove items. A quantity can't exceed the
  product's stock.
- Show the subtotal, an 18% tax and the total. Amounts always display correctly to 2 decimal places.
- The cart survives a page refresh.
- Show an empty-cart state.

## Acceptance criteria

- Changing a quantity updates every total immediately.
- At least one automated test.
